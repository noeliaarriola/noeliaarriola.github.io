03 / 06The first 90 days

Two weeks to diagnose. Then we ship.

No months of strategy decks. Your first acquisition channel goes live in month one.

  1. Days 1–15

    Find the fastest route

    Customer and win/loss interviews, ICP and messaging, a funnel baseline (lead-to-meeting, meeting-to-SAO, win rate) and a list of stalled deals worth reopening.

  2. Days 16–30

    First channel live

    First sequences or campaigns launched, demo-request flow and landing page fixed, tracking live in your CRM.

  3. Days 31–60

    Generate and qualify

    Weekly message and audience tests, opportunities handed to sales with context, and we cut what isn't working.

  4. Days 61–90

    Make it consistent

    Conversion by stage, a written playbook your team can run, and a clear call: scale the channel, add a second one, or hire.

06 / 06Frequently asked questions

What founders usually want to know before a first call.

Are you a lead generation agency?

No. Lead volume is easy to inflate. We build the system that turns the right buyers into opportunities your sales team accepts, and that's what we report on.

Do you guarantee results?

We don't guarantee a number of leads, opportunities or revenue. We commit to a clear scope, a live channel in the first month and honest weekly reporting on the numbers that matter.

How fast will we see results?

The first channel goes live by day 30. How fast it turns into meetings depends on your ACV, sales cycle and channel, so you see the numbers every week from the start instead of waiting for day 90.

Which channels do you run?

The one or two with the best evidence for your buyers and budget. Usually targeted outbound, content, partnerships, search or paid media. Picking fewer channels is part of the method.

Who actually does the work?

Amena Studio is a founder-led boutique. We own diagnosis, strategy, positioning, messaging and coordination, and bring in trusted specialists for CRM, media, web development, data and creative when the scope needs them.

What do you need from us?

Access to the relevant data and tools, a short weekly sync with whoever owns sales, and the capacity to follow up when opportunities arrive. Closing, product and pricing stay with you.

Should we just hire someone in-house?

Often, eventually. A 90-day build gives you a working channel, a playbook and real numbers first, so the person you hire inherits a system instead of a blank page.

What happens after 90 days?

You keep the playbook and assets. Then you decide: keep working with us, take it in-house, or pause. No long lock-in.
